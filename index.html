Privacy Policy for Peletko

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Peletko - Potrošnja peleta mobile application ("Peletko", "the App") handles user information. Peletko is developed and published by ProleApps.

1. Data Collection

Peletko currently does not collect, transmit, or share personal information or sensitive user data.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, address, or precise location.

Information entered by the user for tracking pellet consumption, expenses, statistics, and related calculations is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to ProleApps or any third-party server by the current version of the App.

2. How the App Uses Information

Information entered into Peletko is used solely to provide the application's local functionality, including tracking pellet consumption, calculating expenses, and displaying statistics.

The current version of Peletko does not use personal information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or user tracking.

3. Data Sharing

Peletko currently does not share user data with ProleApps, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

4. Data Storage and Retention

User-entered application data is stored locally on the user's device. ProleApps does not currently maintain a remote copy of this data.

Because the current version does not transmit user data to ProleApps, ProleApps does not have access to or control over locally stored application data. Users can manage or delete locally stored data through the application's available functions or by removing the application's stored data or uninstalling the App, depending on the device and operating system.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of Peletko does not use third-party services for collecting, analyzing, or tracking personal user information.

6. Future Changes

Peletko may introduce additional features in future versions, including optional online services for collecting anonymous or aggregated information about pellet consumption and usage.

If future versions change how user or device data is collected, stored, processed, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated before such changes become applicable, where required.

7. Children's Privacy

Peletko is not specifically designed for children. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Since the current version of Peletko does not transmit personal or sensitive user information to ProleApps or third-party servers, there is no remote personal-data database maintained by ProleApps for this App.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Peletko, you can contact ProleApps at:

Email: proleapps@gmail.com

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

ProleApps may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
